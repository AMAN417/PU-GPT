==================================================
ELIGIBILITY
===========

For B.Tech four-year programmes, the candidate must have
passed 10+2 or an equivalent examination.

Required subjects:
Physics and Mathematics are compulsory.

The candidate must have at least one of the following
subjects:

* Chemistry
* Biotechnology
* Biology
* Technical Vocational subject
* Computer Science
* Information Technology
* Informatics Practices
* Agriculture
* Engineering Graphics
* Business Studies
* Electronics
* Entrepreneurship

Minimum aggregate marks:

General Category:
45%

SC/ST and Disabled Persons Categories:
40%

==================================================
B.TECH CSE ADMISSION PROCESS
============================

The general admission process is:

1. Register on the Punjabi University admission portal.
2. Select the B.Tech Four-Year programme (Form No. 18).
3. Fill the online application/registration form.
4. Provide the required academic and category information.
5. Participate in counselling according to the applicable
   admission criteria.
6. Original documents are verified during counselling.
7. Seat allocation is done according to merit and applicable
   admission rules.
8. If a seat is allocated, the candidate must deposit the
   required first-semester/course fee within the prescribed
   process.
9. After successful verification and fee payment, admission
   is confirmed according to university rules.

==================================================
ADMISSION / MERIT CRITERIA
==========================

For B.Tech CSE at Punjabi University Main Campus, the
2026 counselling information included:

1. JEE Main merit-based counselling.
2. 10+2 based admission using Physics + Mathematics +
   third-subject merit, according to the notified criteria.

The third subject may be one of:

* Chemistry
* Biotechnology
* Biology
* Technical Vocational subject
* Computer Science
* Information Technology
* Informatics Practices
* Agriculture
* Engineering Graphics
* Business Studies
* Electronics
* Entrepreneurship

The exact counselling schedule and applicable merit criteria
may change by counselling round.

PU-GPT must use the latest verified university notice when
answering questions about current counselling dates or merit.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR COUNSELLING
==================================

The official B.Tech CSE counselling instructions list the

1. 10th DMC and Date of Birth Certificate

==================================================
FEE PAYMENT AT COUNSELLING
==========================

For the 2026-27 B.Tech CSE Main Campus counselling instructions:

Course Fee:
₹64,755

SAF Fund:
₹1,000

SAF is compulsory for admitted candidates and is
non-refundable.

Special SC/ST provision:

SC/ST applicants who are Punjab residents and whose family
income satisfies the Post Matric Scholarship norms must
deposit ₹2,000 as specified by the university, with a valid
income certificate.

The official counselling instructions state that the
required fee must be deposited at counselling for seat
confirmation.

The document specifies payment through separate Demand Drafts
for the applicable course fee and SAF.

Cheque payment is not accepted according to the counselling
instructions.

==================================================
SEAT ALLOCATION
===============

Seat allocation is based on merit according to the notified
admission criteria.

Candidates must report according to the counselling schedule.

Late reporting may result in seat allocation only according
to seats available at that time and subject to eligibility.

Incomplete or inappropriate documents can prevent seat
allotment.
