==================================================
HOSTEL FACILITIES
=================

Punjabi University provides residential hostel facilities
for students.

According to the official Punjabi University website:

* There are residential hostels for boys.
* There are residential hostels for girls.
* Guru Kashi Campus, Talwandi Sabo has separate hostel
  facilities for boys and girls.
* Punjabi University Neighbourhood Campus, Rampura Phul has
  separate hostel facilities for boys and girls.
* University College of Engineering has its own separate
  hostel facility.

The university states that its hostels have Wi-Fi and
CCTV camera facilities.

==================================================
B.TECH / ENGINEERING STUDENTS
=============================

University College of Engineering has its own separate
hostel facility.

Students admitted to engineering programmes may obtain
hostel accommodation subject to the university's hostel
rules, availability and allocation process.

Hostel accommodation is NOT automatically guaranteed merely
because a student has received admission to a course.

==================================================
HOSTEL ALLOCATION
=================

Hostel allocation is subject to:

* Availability of hostel seats
* University hostel rules
* Applicable merit/allocation criteria
* Completion of the required hostel admission process
* Payment of applicable hostel charges

Students should follow the latest hostel notice issued by
Punjabi University for the relevant academic session.
